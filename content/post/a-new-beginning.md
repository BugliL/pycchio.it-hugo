This is a beginning of a new project. In my job I need to improve my knowledge,
to embrace new technologies and to be able to adapt to the changes. I want to
share some of it, small pills of knowledge, that I will learn along the way. 

I'm a software engineer, and I have been working in the industry for a few years
now. I have learned a lot, but I know that there is still so much to learn and
to discover. I know that at work there's no too much time and space to learn
something brand new, it's too risky or too time-consuming. I want to make an
effort to learn something new every week, even if it's just a small thing, and
that's why I'm forcing myself to write about it and to study it in depth.

I had an old blog, made with **Jekyll**, but I never really used it.  I want to give
it a new life, and I want to do it with **Hugo**, a static site generator that I
find very interesting to introduce myself to **Go** programming language. I want to
learn how to use it, how to customize it, and how to make it my own.

Let's see how it goes!
